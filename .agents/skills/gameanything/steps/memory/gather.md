# Step gather — 放进素材 (her)
Entry: a new memory project. Nothing to sort yet.
Do: greet her in one line and ask her to put the day into the 素材 page (photos, videos, voice notes, invitations, chat screenshots, a few lines of what happened), then click 「整理新文件」. A folder path in the chat works too.
Card: none needed. If she asks what to upload, answer in text and point to 素材 (`#gather`): the more photos of the people and places, the closer the world.
Exit: files are in 0_inbox or already ingested.
Mistakes: asking her to paste everything into the chat; starting the day from your own guesses; sending anything to a model before she agreed.
