Entry: the task card to 内容 is out. She goes card by card and clicks 「确认内容」 (gameplay_review.yaml `content: {state: confirmed}`).
While you wait: her notes arrive as chat lines (`card:<id>` etc.): change gameplay.yaml in that run, keep the ids, say what changed.
Tell her in one line where to confirm.
Do the 「等她的时候可以先做」 items of `gameanything flow next` (English fields, the BRIEF draft) while she reads.
Exit: content confirmed.
