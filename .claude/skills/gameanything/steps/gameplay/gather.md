# Step gather — 说说经历 (her)
Entry: a new gameplay project (game: usually slay_the_spire).
Do: greet her in one line and ask her to tell the experience (a PhD, a startup, raising a child, exam prep…): in the chat, or as text / design notes on the 经历 page, then 「整理新文件」. Her own design notes (a mapping table, cards, enemies) are the best input.
Card: none needed yet.
Exit: she said something in the chat, or files are in 经历.
Mistakes: designing from your own idea of the experience before she said anything; asking for names of real people.
