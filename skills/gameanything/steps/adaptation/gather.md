# Step gather — 放进作品 (her)
Entry: a new adaptation project. Nothing to extract yet.
Do: greet her in one line and ask her to put the work into the 作品 page (txt / md / pdf / epub, subtitles, scripts, stills, her own video clips), then click 「整理新文件」. Only files she legally owns; never download books or media for her.
Card: none needed. If she asks what to upload, answer in text and point to 作品 (`#gather`).
Exit: files are in 0_inbox or already ingested.
Mistakes: asking her to paste a whole novel into the chat; downloading a copy yourself; starting extraction from your own memory of the work.
