# Step gather — 说想加什么 (her)
Entry: a new feature project.
Do: greet her in one line and ask what she wants to add to her game (a dish, an item, a mini game, a place), in a sentence or two; photos or references can go into 素材 and she clicks 「整理新文件」.
Card: none needed yet (the questions come next).
Her words are kept verbatim in feedback/answers.md; your reading of them is a separate line she confirms later.
Exit: she said something in the chat (or files are in 素材).
Mistakes: starting a plan before she said what she wants; asking many questions in plain text (they go into one questions card next).
