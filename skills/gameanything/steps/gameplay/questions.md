# Step questions — 问关键问题 (you, then her)
Entry: her words or notes are read.
Do: retell the core tension in one or two lines (e.g. long investment vs uncertain feedback), then ONE `questions` card in this run about what only she knows: what a day looks like, the hardest and the proudest moments, inside jokes, what players should feel, what must NOT go in (sensitive things, specific people). Each item with a `hint`; partial answers are fine.
```yaml
kind: questions
key: gameplay-questions
title: 读完你的笔记，有 5 个问题（可以只答一部分）
items:
  - {n: 1, text: 每天大部分时间在做什么？, hint: 默认：读论文、跑实验、改 Bug、写作}
en: {title: "5 questions after reading your notes (answer any)"}
```
Exit: card sent, hints everywhere, she answered.
Mistakes: plain-text questions; asking what her notes already say.
