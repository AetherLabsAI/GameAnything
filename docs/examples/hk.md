# Hong Kong Food — a cha chaan teng kitchen in Overcooked! 2

**Mode:** Feature · **Game:** Overcooked! 2 (new level mod)
**What came in:** my food photos from Hong Kong, and a wish: classic Hong Kong dishes that the game doesn't already have, a Hong Kong map, and two chefs with local flavour.
**What came out:** four versions. The first was a reskin (new pictures on old recipes), and I said so. Version 2 cooked every dish the real way, version 3 put 3D dishes on the plates, and version 4 fixed two things I spotted in the video.

**What this example shows:** in Feature mode you add something new to a game: here, a level, eight dishes and two chefs. The agent says early what the game can and can't do. When version 1 was only a reskin, one blunt note ("the cooking is all wrong") reset the plan to real methods. From then on, my short notes on the video ("what's this pink thing at 0:52?") became the next fix list.
